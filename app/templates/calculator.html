Investment Analysis

Enter property details and click Calculate to see the analysis.

Gross Yield

Annual rent divided by purchase price. A good BTL yield in the UK is typically 5-8%. Higher yields often indicate higher risk areas.

Cash Flow

Monthly rent minus mortgage payment. Positive cash flow means the property pays for itself. Consider adding 10-15% for maintenance and voids.

ROI on Deposit

Annual profit divided by your cash investment (deposit). This measures how hard your money is working for you.
